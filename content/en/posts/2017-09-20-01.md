This is a Korean university in Ansan. I did not manage to explore all of it because it is very large, with parks and big buildings.

![The university grounds in Ansan](/images/2017-09-20-01-02.webp)

![One of the university buildings](/images/2017-09-20-01-03.webp)

As it turns out, Koreans are not as short as I thought. The average height of a Korean man is 173–176 cm, while for women it is 166–172 cm. However, men who are 185 cm or taller and women who are 175 cm or taller are also often seen. There are Chinese people here too, and the difference is noticeable in their height — they are about a head shorter than Koreans.

![A pathway on the university grounds](/images/2017-09-20-01-04.webp)

![A green area of the university campus](/images/2017-09-20-01-05.webp)

Koreans are obsessed with appearance. Everyone uses cosmetics, and, as I have heard from others more than once, the men here are actually more attractive than the women because they are better groomed. Women usually wear baggy sweaters or loose T-shirts. Many women also wear very short shorts.

![The university campus in Ansan](/images/2017-09-20-01-06.webp)

![A view of the university buildings](/images/2017-09-20-01-07.webp)

Koreans are very neat. My sister told me to keep my clothes clean because dirty or wrinkled clothing is considered a sign of low social status or simply untidiness.

![Walking around the university grounds](/images/2017-09-20-01-08.webp)

![Trees near the university buildings](/images/2017-09-20-01-09.webp)

By the way, while I was talking, a Korean woman sitting by the window to my right fell asleep. She is now sleeping while leaning on the table by the windowsill. 😂 It looks like they study a lot. The music here is also very calming and soothing… It is interesting that no one else is paying attention. I suppose this is normal here :)))
