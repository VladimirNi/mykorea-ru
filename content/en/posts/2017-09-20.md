I’m sitting in a café called Coffee Planet, located near the university campus. There are lots of students here. A couple sitting to my left are playing chess, while two students by the window are using their laptops.

![A display case with cakes and desserts](/images/2017-09-20-02.webp)

The cake is delicious! The atmosphere is pleasant… It feels fresh and smells wonderful… A nice, soft breeze is coming through the window… You can see green trees outside.

![Drinks on a café table](/images/2017-09-20-03.webp)

![A slice of cake and a drink](/images/2017-09-20-05.webp)

For some reason, Koreans take off their shoes when they sit in cafés and sit cross-legged… Some put one leg up, but they still take off their shoes… By the way, it is considered bad manners here when someone has an unpleasant smell, so Koreans use special products. My sister immediately gave me foot cream and mint candies :)

![Café cards and a business card](/images/2017-09-20-04.webp)
