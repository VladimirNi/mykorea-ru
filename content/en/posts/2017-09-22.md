Kindergarten 😊

They are well equipped both outside and inside. The teachers are young women with the appropriate professional education. Special buses bring the children to kindergarten and take them home. The monthly cost is about $300 per child. This is not expensive for Koreans, considering that an average family earns around $7,000.

![The grounds around the kindergarten](/images/2017-09-22-02.webp)

P.S. I’m at LOTTE MART now :) I reached it while walking around and exploring the city… At the moment, I’m sitting inside a cinema auditorium. The Wi-Fi here is free and very fast.

I’ll only be able to get a phone in early November, after I receive my ID card. They already have 5G internet here :) I was offered an unlimited mobile plan and a discounted iPhone 7 Plus :)
