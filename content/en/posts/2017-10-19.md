I was walking around Myeongdong and looking at some meat patties... The seller showed me two options and said, “Spicy — No Spicy.” I thought, what kind of silly question is that... Of course I want the one with spices—why would I eat an unseasoned kebab... “Diet food,” I thought, and chose the “Spicy” one.

I got home, called my friend Katya and told her... “Today I ate these really tasty meat patties, but damn, they were so hot!” I explained that there were ones with spices and without spices, Spicy and No Spicy... And she goes, “What are you doooing! Spicy doesn’t mean ‘with spices’—spicy means hot!” Well, at least we had a good laugh 😂
