I’m at a Japanese sushi 🍣 bar. Damn, the prices are crazy... she said $5 gets you two little pieces of “sushi.” They talked me into getting a set for $18. I don’t know what they’ll bring yet. But if it isn’t a whole basin of sushi, I’m never setting foot in a Japanese restaurant again 😅

Oh damn... these are the most delicious sushi I’ve ever had in my life! 💖✨ Real fish, miso soup to wash it down with (there’s no tea, only sake), and this breaded fish... it just melts in your mouth! When I put the first sushi 🍣 in my mouth, honestly, I sat there for about 10 seconds with my eyes wide open... My brain froze 😅 I didn’t expect it to be this delicious!

I got another portion to take away! 😭 Now I think $18 for something like this is really cheap 😅
