Many small shops have special places where you can eat what you’ve just bought. Koreans value their time, so this kind of service seems to have appeared naturally. There’s a microwave, a hot-water dispenser, and on the right a coffee machine with three kinds of coffee: Americano, iced Americano and cappuccino.
