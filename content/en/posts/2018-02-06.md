Dinner at Kiyong’s house... I ended up at this dinner because I didn’t understand English properly 😅

I woke up in the evening after a daytime nap and read a Kakao message from Kiyong: “Did Taeun call you?” Then I read Taeun’s messages: “Dinner with Kiyong has changed...” and he added "this evening". I thought, okay... if it’s on the weekend, then it’s on the weekend... I’d only just woken up, I was sleepy and didn’t want meat, so I figured I’d go buy grapes and apples instead...

Then Taeun called and asked me to come to Akashi House. I thought they probably felt guilty because they’d changed the plans without telling me, so maybe they’d decided to have dinner at Akashi House... I’d go and tell them I was on a diet and wouldn’t be able to come to dinner on the weekend... and if there was food at Akashi House, it would probably be fruit, and I wanted some tangerines anyway... everything seemed perfect. Still in my home pajamas, I threw on a jacket, put on slippers and headed to Akashi House. I went in and looked for Taeun in the classroom, but there were only three schoolkids there. I went down to the meeting room and even the lights were off. As I passed the entrance, some Korean guy in an SUV honked... I thought it was because of traffic and ignored it... Then Taeun called and shouted, “Vova, I’m here... could you please get into my car?” I wondered where he was planning to take me... I got in, and he said, “The plans changed, the meeting will be ‘this evening’...” I replied, “Yeah, it’s all good... I already know... but where are we going now?” He said, “To Kiyong’s... this dinner is especially for you...” And then it finally hit me... evening means вечер, while выходные is “weekend” (facepalm) 😅

There was a very tasty meat soup... it was light. And lots of meat... first one portion, then another whole mountain of meat... kimchi (very tasty) and a salad of fresh fruit with sour cream... really delicious! And on the right there’s a cup with toy fruit—Kiyong’s three-year-old son prepared dinner for me! 😂 So cute) He liked me 💖🤗 We played with his toys together )))

So, I didn’t manage to lose weight today... I stuffed myself... I decided that tomorrow I’ll tell them I have a lot of work right up until November, so I won’t be able to accept their invitations. No joke, I’m already tired of eating... in these amounts... they don’t gain weight, but I’m going to turn into a little round bun. Better if I tell you about their culture and everyday life instead (excluding food) and show you the nature...

Learn English well! Then you won’t end up in situations like mine.... 😅 Dress warmly and eat well! 🙄💖✨
