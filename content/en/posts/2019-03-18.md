In the morning I decided to relax outdoors... on a platform... I went into the restroom and saw a big red 🆘 button and a speaker... I thought, wow, that’s a really good idea... then I wondered, why is this in the men’s restroom...? o_O Are Korean women really such perverts that they chase their oppas into restrooms... well, there actually are slim, cute, defenseless-looking guys here... and there have been cases where women were prosecuted for sexually harassing men. Anyway, I thought I’d photograph the button later and show you... and went on with my business... I walked farther in and couldn’t understand it: stalls on the right and stalls on the left, but where are the urinals? That’s when I realized I had walked into the women’s restroom... 😱 Good thing nobody saw me and nobody was inside, otherwise I might have had to explain myself to the police, whew... I wanted to go back and photograph the button, but figured that if there are cameras there (which is very likely), they’d definitely think something bad about me. So, be careful when you’re in Korea...
