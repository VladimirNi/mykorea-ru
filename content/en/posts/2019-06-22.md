Good morning everyone! ✨☁️😊
Sorry I disappeared after my last message about the police station. Last week was very eventful 😊

First of all, congratulate me: I finally got my driver’s license! 🥳🥳🥳 I took the tests without really knowing how to drive. I passed the theory on the first try, the closed-course test on the fifth, and the road test also on the fifth try. The instructors were very strict. For even the smallest violation, they made you get out immediately. Even if the monitor showed 100 points, the instructor could cancel the result. Safety comes first. If they see that you’re not ready, they don’t let you pass. Fair enough. And of course, they don’t take bribes 😊

Now about the police station... I lost a vest with my documents in it 😅 and the police looked for it... the guys there were nice, I think they liked me 😅 When I was leaving, I also forgot my umbrella there, and one officer ran after me to return it.... 😂 They probably thought, “No wonder he lost his vest” 😂 They found the taxi, and the taxi driver came back from far away and returned it to me. He refused to take money. There was no paperwork... they simply gave it back and waved goodbye... strange, they could have logged it as their work and I would have written a thank-you note.... I’ll buy them pizza later) good guys, it was fun... even though it was already after midnight... )

Learn Korean! They told me... if you’re planning to live in Korea, you need to learn Korean... I promised I definitely would) and you should learn too! Not everyone understands English) and they appreciate it more when you try to speak Korean, even if it’s not very good.

Anyway, as always, I live an entertaining life... I manage to find adventures out of nowhere.

And recently I also lost my card. A woman called me and said my card was at the police station...

I need to be more organized )))
