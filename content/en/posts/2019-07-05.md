When I often walk through parks, I see Koreans sleeping on benches or on the grass... turns out it’s very convenient... when home is far away and the fresh air in the shade makes you sleepy, you can just take a nap... today I was riding through a park too, sat down to rest, then lay down and fell asleep... I would have slept longer, but I had things to do... so I got on my bicycle, still sleepy, and rode on...
