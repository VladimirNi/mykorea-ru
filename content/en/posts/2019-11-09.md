I went into a shop to replace the arms on my glasses... one of them had gone missing... I asked if they had replacement arms, and a young guy nodded and immediately took my glasses to install one... then I asked if they had a cloth for glasses... he went and got one... then I asked how much everything cost... he said I didn’t need to pay anything, it was just free... I thought maybe I wasn’t understanding something... asked again, and he again said I didn’t need to pay... I took out money and said, “I don’t understand... how much for everything?” He started waving his hands: it’s just free... I asked, “A gift?” He said, “Yes, yes, a gift...” I smiled broadly: “Aaa... got it... thank you!” I probably cheered them up with how surprised I was 😂 it’s a small thing, but nice... 🤗
