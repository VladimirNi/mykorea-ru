In the morning, a friend of mine (a local Korean) called and invited me to eat at a Chinese restaurant. Her boyfriend paid for everything. He said that since he was older, he would pay. We practically rolled out of there like little balls... the portions turned out to be huge! We ordered four dishes, and they also gave us a big plate of salads as a complimentary service.

1. Asparagus and tofu
2. Meat sliced into noodle-like strips
3. Tirgumi, noodles and seafood
4. Chicken in a spicy-sweet sauce
5. Soup with noodles, egg and kimchi... spicy, but tasty)
