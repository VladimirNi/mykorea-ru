Seoul and nearby cities introduced Level 2.5 distancing, so in sikdangs (eateries) you have to sit wearing a mask until the food is brought. Then you eat and put the mask back on. It looks strange, but Koreans strictly follow the government’s recommendations.

1. A rich soup
2. A bonus with the soup
3. This meat is very tasty, but not everyone will like it; all the meat here is pork 😅
4. A minced-meat sausage — I don’t know what it’s made of, and the taste is so-so
5. Social responsibility 😂👍🏻
