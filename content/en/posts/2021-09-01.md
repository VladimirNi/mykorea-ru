I had to take a test. My Korean teacher called and said that COVID had been confirmed in the family of one of her students. It turned out she found out yesterday, took a test, and then had a lesson with me. I don’t understand why she didn’t tell me before the lesson yesterday... today she called to say that her test was negative, while her student’s was positive. Irresponsible behavior. Luckily I hadn’t been in contact with my family. I’ll wait for the result.

And one more thing: when they took the sample, they mixed up the digits 8 and 0, so according to the tube I’m 15 years old 😅
