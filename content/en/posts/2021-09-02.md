Korea pays compensation to everyone who gets sick (or is forced to quarantine because of contact with someone who has COVID). On average, the payments are around $400 per person; in the comments, people wrote about already receiving 1.2–1.4 million won (1 million won is about 63,000 rubles).

P.S. My test was negative. That’s good... of course, it’s better not to get sick at all!
