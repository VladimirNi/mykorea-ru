A little tradition with my landlords... since the beginning of the pandemic, the owners of the house where I rent my apartment — an elderly couple — have been giving 10,000 won back from my rent. Every time they say, “Go eat at a sikdang.” There’s no escaping it: they knock on my door and hand me the money 😅 They probably think I’m having a hard time because I sometimes forget to pay the electricity bill.
