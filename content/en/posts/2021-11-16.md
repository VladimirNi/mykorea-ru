Street food... I took a lot, and together with the chicken it came to 13,000 won... I couldn’t finish it, so I asked them to pack it up. Street food usually comes to 5–6 thousand won when you get fish cakes with broth and sliced liver. But here everything was battered... cheese sausages, crab sticks, fried zucchini and sweet potato, king prawns, dumplings and something with nori too... all with spicy tteokbokki sauce 🤗
