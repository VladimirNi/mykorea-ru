Do you remember the heroine of the drama "Kill Me, Heal Me"? Her name is Hwang Jung Eum. She was giving away her children’s toys, so my wife and I went to get an Iron Man figure (for my inner child 😅).

![A close-up of Hwang Jung Eum](/images/2026-02-16-02.webp)

She showed me where everything was, then asked where we were from... I didn’t recognize her right away and went to choose toys ) Then I heard her bright laugh — exactly like in "Kill Me, Heal Me". Amazing. I thought that was just part of her character, but apparently not )))

![An Iron Man toy](/images/2026-02-16-03.webp)

We took a photo together, picked up some hats for our summer vacation when we go to Busan, and when we were already leaving, she came over and gave me Lash hand cream and said, "It’s a gift" 😊

![A straw hat](/images/2026-02-16-04.webp)

Kind and attentive — she made a very good impression )

![A jar of hand cream](/images/2026-02-16-05.webp)

P.S. The hats really are great! We have bucket hats, but they don’t protect from the sun that well... these are exactly what we need! =)
