Dear Readers,

It has been a while since our last update, but we are back with some exciting news. We are officially relaunching the site with a fresh vision!

You can expect posts focused entirely on my personal experiences living in South Korea, along with practical tips and insights gained from daily life here. Stay tuned—new stories are coming very soon.

Don't worry, we haven't deleted anything. All the previous articles and contributions from our guest bloggers have been safely migrated to our archive.

> 📂 **Access the Archive:** You can find the legacy version of the site at [archive.mykorea.ru](http://archive.mykorea.ru)
